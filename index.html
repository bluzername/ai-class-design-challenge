AI helper tips

Use ChatGPT smartly

🧠

1. Ask for choices, not one answer

Prompt: "Give us 5 AI class ideas for kids aged 9-13. Make them fun, safe, and possible in 1 hour."

🎚️

2. Set clear limits

Tell the chatbot your time, tools, and level. Good limits give better ideas.

🔁

3. Ask it to improve one part

Example: "Make step 2 more active," or "simplify this for students with weaker English."

🧪

4. Use it to test your plan

Prompt: "What could go wrong with this class idea? Give 3 problems and 3 fixes."
